All of these codes were developed for the research work 
"Modeling of rotor faults in squirrel cage induction motors," funded by
the Strategy Research Development Fund of GIZ (Deutsche Gesellschaft für
Internationale Zusammenarbeit), the German Agency for International
Cooperation in Mongolia. The results of this research study in the
paper under the topic "Determination of Optimal Parameters of Simulated
Motors Based on the Grey Relational Analysis" will be published in t has been 
submitted to the journal Proceedings of "2020 IEEE International Conference
on Power and Energy (PECon2020).".

To get results, call the scripts file named  "Call_allFunctionFiles.m"
 to the Command Window and run it. Then all results come out.
